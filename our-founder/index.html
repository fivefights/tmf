Founder

Lakeisha Sesay, MPA

Founder & Managing Director

You don't need another public sector advisor who only knows what should happen. You need one who knows exactly what it takes to make it happen inside a real public-sector organization — and create the momentum required to carry it through.

Lakeisha Sesay, MPA, is an award-winning public sector strategist, public policy advisor, university instructor, and management consulting executive. She has spent her career inside the kinds of organizations The Momentum Firm now serves: government, nonprofits, higher education, foundations, multilateral organizations, and global consulting.

That experience taught her early that the idea is only one facet of the work. The real challenge is carrying it through an organization with competing priorities, finite resources, complex decision-making, and people balancing transformation with everything already on their plates. She brings strategic thinking grounded in how public-sector organizations actually operate, and a practical instinct for what it takes to turn an ambitious priority into something an organization can deliver.

Before founding The Momentum Firm, Lakeisha was a North America Public Sector Strategy Consulting Executive at Accenture. She advised senior public administrators and elected officials across the United States, Canada, and Mexico on strategy and technology, and led complex, high-stakes transformation programs on their behalf. As Accenture's Atlanta Corporate Citizenship Lead, she also created the Atlanta Corporate Recycling Challenge, a competition among the city's largest corporations that has diverted more than 30 million pounds of waste from landfills.

Her work has taken her to more than six countries, with organizations including the United Nations, the European Union, the U.S. Centers for Disease Control and Prevention, state and local governments, and community-based organizations. Across all of it, she has aligned strategy, technology, innovation, and human-centered design to deliver results that matter — for organizations and for the communities they serve.

Lakeisha teaches applied research and innovation in management consulting as a Visiting Instructor at Georgia State University's Robinson College of Business, and serves on the board of the Captain Planet Foundation. She holds a Master of Public Administration with honors from Kennesaw State University, which named her a 40 Under 40 honoree, and a B.A. in International Studies from Georgia Southern University. She is a distinguished alumna of Leadership Atlanta's LEAD Class of 2024. A first-generation Sierra Leonean-American born and raised in Georgia, she lives between Atlanta and Washington, D.C.

She founded The Momentum Firm so public-sector leaders would have a partner who understands the system from the inside, sees where there's room to rethink it, and knows how to carry bold thinking all the way through to implementation. That's still what drives her: helping leaders create momentum around the priorities that matter, and turning that momentum into visible progress for the people and communities they serve.
