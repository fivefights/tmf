About Us

Empowering change, sustaining results

The Momentum Firm is an end-to-end management consultancy specializing in transforming governments, the public sector, and social impact organizations around the world.

Founder

Lakeisha Sesay, MPA

Founder & Managing Director

The Momentum Firm was founded by Lakeisha Sesay, MPA, award-winning government and public sector strategist, public policy advisor, university instructor and management consulting expert.

Prior to founding The Momentum Firm, Lakeisha served as a North America Strategy Executive for the Public Service Industry at Accenture, where she gained a highly reputable track record of successfully leading complex and high-stakes transformation programs on behalf of c-suite public sector leaders and elected officials.

Her work has consistently delivered results that matter for both organizations and communities — successfully aligning strategy, technology, innovation, and human-centered design to deliver lasting value.

Our Mission

Ensuring public sector and social impact leaders have the cutting-edge strategies, tools, and support to perform at their best, because when they perform, communities prosper.

We exist to close the gap between intention and impact. That means rejecting templated approaches in favor of strategies that are tailored, human-centered, agile, and innovation-led.

With proven, multidisciplinary experience across public sector industries, and an insider's grasp of the nuances leaders face (the politics, the pressures, the trade-offs), we design solutions that go beyond solving problems. They build resilience, open new possibilities, and leave organizations and communities stronger than before.

Our Vision

To enable proactive public sector organizations where innovation isn't an afterthought, but the standard, and where momentum drives both meaningful and tangible outcomes for the people they serve.

We believe the public sector should do more than react to change. They should shape it entirely. By blending cutting-edge strategies, modern practices, and a commitment to visible impact, we envision a future where public service organizations lead with confidence, operate with precision and efficiency, and deliver perceptible progress that people can see and feel in their everyday lives.
