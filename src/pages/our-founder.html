Founder Bio

Lakeisha Sesay, MPA

Founder & Managing Director

Lakeisha Sesay, MPA, is an award-winning public sector strategist, policy advisor, university instructor, and management consulting executive. Having worked across government, nonprofits, higher education, foundations, and multilateral institutions, she understands firsthand that strategy is only one piece of the puzzle. The real challenge is executing bold priorities within organizations navigating competing demands, finite resources, and complex governance. She founded The Momentum Firm to give public-service and social-impact leaders a partner who knows the system from the inside and knows how to turn ambitious vision into visible progress for the communities and constituents they serve.

Before founding The Momentum Firm, Lakeisha was a North America Strategy Executive for the Public Service industry at Accenture, advising senior administrators and elected officials across the U.S., Canada, and Mexico on large-scale strategy and technology transformations. Her global consulting portfolio spans more than six countries with organizations including the United Nations, the European Union, the CDC, and multiple state and local governments.

Lakeisha is a Visiting Instructor at Georgia State University's Robinson College of Business and serves on the Board of Visitors for Kennesaw State's Radow College of Social Sciences as well as on the board of the Captain Planet Foundation. She holds an MPA with honors from Kennesaw State University, which named her a 40 Under 40 honoree, and a B.A. in International Studies from Georgia Southern University.
