Federal Government

Federal agencies carry missions that reach every community in the country. We help them modernize how they operate and deliver, without losing sight of the mission or the people it serves.

The Landscape

Modernization at national scale.

Agencies are balancing legacy systems, evolving mandates, workforce transitions, and the push to adopt AI responsibly, all under oversight from Congress, inspectors general, and the public.

Large programs involve many vendors and stakeholders, and small coordination gaps can become costly delays. Success depends on disciplined delivery, clear governance, and services designed around the people who use them.*

Where We Focus

The priorities we help move forward.

Mission Program Delivery

We provide client-side leadership for major initiatives, with integrated planning, risk management, and executive reporting that keep complex programs on schedule and on budget.*

Technology Modernization Oversight

We act as an independent advocate through system implementations, holding vendors accountable and surfacing risk early so modernization delivers what was promised.*

Responsible AI & Data

We help agencies build the data governance and readiness AI requires, identify high-value use cases, and put guardrails in place before scaling.*

Workforce & Change

We prepare employees for new systems and ways of working through change planning, training, and adoption support grounded in federal workforce realities.*
