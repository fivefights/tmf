Intergovernmental Organizations

When multiple governments share a mission, alignment is the work. We help intergovernmental organizations govern, coordinate, and deliver shared programs.

The Landscape

Many members, one mission.

Intergovernmental organizations, from regional councils of government to international bodies, coordinate members with different priorities, funding cycles, and political pressures. Decisions take consensus, and delivery often depends on partners they don't control.

Clear governance, shared measures of success, and programs designed to work across jurisdictions turn collaboration into results. Our team's experience includes work with organizations such as the United Nations and the European Union.*

Where We Focus

The priorities we help move forward.

Governance & Member Alignment

We design governance structures, decision rights, and charters that help members set shared priorities and move from discussion to decisions.*

Shared Program Delivery

We plan and manage multi-jurisdiction programs with integrated plans, clear roles, and reporting that keeps every partner accountable.*

Shared Data & Measurement

We build common performance frameworks and data standards so members can track collective progress and demonstrate impact to funders and the public.*

Stakeholder Engagement

We design engagement that brings member governments, partners, and communities into the process, building trust across borders and sectors.*
