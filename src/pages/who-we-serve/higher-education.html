Higher Education

Colleges and universities are adapting to shifting enrollment, new expectations, and tighter budgets. We help them operate with clarity and serve students better.

The Landscape

Institutions in transition.

Higher education faces demographic shifts, financial pressure, questions about value, and the rapid arrival of AI, alongside students who expect seamless digital services.

Shared governance, decentralized structures, and long-standing traditions make change complex. Progress depends on clear priorities, good data, and the trust of faculty, staff, and students.*

Where We Focus

The priorities we help move forward.

Institutional Strategy & Planning

We facilitate strategic plans that build consensus across shared governance and translate into resourced, measurable priorities.*

Organizational & Operational Design

We assess administrative structures and processes, and redesign them to reduce duplication and free resources for teaching, research, and student success.*

Student Experience & Digital Services

We research and redesign the student journey, from admissions to advising, and build digital services students actually want to use.*

Data, Analytics & AI Readiness

We help institutions govern their data, build dashboards for leaders, and prepare responsibly for AI across campus.*
