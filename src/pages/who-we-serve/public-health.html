Public Health

Public health agencies protect communities every day, often out of sight until a crisis. We help them strengthen programs, data, and operations so they're ready for what comes next.

The Landscape

Ready for every day, and for the next crisis.

Health departments are rebuilding after the pandemic amid workforce shortages, aging data systems, the end of emergency funding, and declining public trust.

Meeting community needs requires modern data infrastructure, programs designed with the people they serve, and the operational discipline to deliver at community scale.*

Where We Focus

The priorities we help move forward.

Data Modernization

We help agencies strengthen data governance, integrate systems, and build dashboards that turn surveillance and program data into timely action.*

Program Delivery & Management

We provide program management for major initiatives and grant-funded programs, keeping complex efforts on track and accountable.*

Workforce & Organizational Change

We help departments plan workforce transitions, redesign structures, and support staff through new systems and ways of working.*

Community Engagement & Trust

We design engagement and communication that bring communities into decisions and help rebuild trust in public health.*
