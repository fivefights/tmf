Corporate Social Responsibility

Companies are expected to show up for the communities where they operate. We help corporate social responsibility teams build programs that align with the business, partner well with the public and nonprofit sectors, and deliver measurable community impact.

The Landscape

Community commitments that hold up to scrutiny.

Employees, investors, and communities now expect companies to back their commitments with action and evidence. Giving, volunteering, and community programs are often spread across business units, with different goals and little shared measurement.

Leading companies connect their community investments to a clear strategy, partner with public agencies and nonprofits that know the ground, and report results in terms the community recognizes.

  • Analytics & Operational Intelligence→Impact measurement and reporting frameworks that turn activity into credible results.
  • Program & Implementation Management→Management of multi-partner community initiatives, from launch through delivery.
  • Organizational Change Management→Employee engagement and community partnership planning that build lasting relationships.
