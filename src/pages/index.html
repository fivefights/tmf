Beyond the strategy

Big public-sector ambitions need more than a plan.

You're being asked to modernize outdated systems. Improve services. Do more with finite resources. Navigate changing administrations and expectations. Introduce new technology without losing the people expected to use it.

And while the mandate may be clear, the implementation strategy rarely is.

The Momentum Firm works alongside public-sector leaders to turn complexity into coordinated action — bringing the right people, priorities, processes, and technology together around the outcomes that matter. So instead of constantly pushing against the same roadblocks, you can finally move the needle from we should to we did with stronger execution and confidence that the change will hold up in the real world.

Success measured by impact

When the public sector thrives, so does the community.

Every improvement inside a public institution eventually ripples outwards to someone — the resident waiting for an answer, the family trying to access support, the student depending on a program or the team delivering the service.

We measure success by what changes on that side of the work; in the everyday experience of the people and communities relying on it.

Lakeisha Sesay, MPA

Founder & Managing Director

The Momentum Firm was founded by Lakeisha Sesay, MPA, award-winning government and public sector strategist, public policy advisor, university instructor and management consulting expert.
