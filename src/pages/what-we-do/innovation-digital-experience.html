Innovation & Digital Experience

Residents expect government services to work as well as the best apps on their phones. Too often they meet paper forms, dated portals, and processes that live in spreadsheets and inboxes, while staff work around tools that were never designed for them. The opportunity is real: a well-designed digital service saves time on both sides of the counter and builds trust every time it works.

What We Offer

Services

We redesign systems, services, and experiences to be more effective for the people who use them. Using human-centered design, rapid prototyping, and evaluation, we help clients test new ideas before scaling them, modernize legacy workflows and constituent services, and design and build the digital products that deliver them. Our team has shipped products used by tens of millions of people, built for accessibility and measurable adoption from day one.

311 & Constituent Services Modernization

We modernize 311 and constituent services end to end: omnichannel strategy, service request workflows, knowledge bases, CRM requirements, and service standards. We also use 311 data to spot trends and predict demand, so the first point of contact becomes a source of insight for the whole government.

Digital Product & Usability Design

We design digital services with real users: research, journey mapping, wireframes, prototypes, and usability testing, with accessibility (WCAG) and plain language built in from the start. We deliver design specifications your developers or vendors can build from, and iterate based on how people actually use the product.

Web & Mobile Product Design

We design end-to-end experiences across web, iOS, and Android, from information architecture and wireframes to high-fidelity prototypes and developer-ready specs. We measure success by outcomes like digital adoption and task completion, not just how it looks. Our track record of technological implementations has lifted digital adoption by a third on average.

Web Application Development

We build fast, modern, maintainable web applications with React, TypeScript, Node.js, GraphQL, and cloud services, backed by automated testing and continuous delivery. We work alongside your IT team or vendors, document what we build, and design for performance and long-term ownership.

Innovation Sprints & Design Workshops

We run design thinking workshops and time-boxed innovation sprints that take teams from problem framing through research, ideation, and rapid prototyping in days or weeks, not months. Teams leave with tested concepts, clear next steps, and the skills to repeat the process themselves.

New Program Design & Pilots

We co-design programs with communities and frontline staff, define the logic model and success measures, and launch through structured pilots that test assumptions before full rollout. You get a program blueprint, operating procedures, and pilot results that make the case for scaling.

Program Evaluation & Improvement

We design and conduct program evaluations, from logic models and evaluation plans to data collection, analysis, and findings, using methods suited to the program and its budget. Every evaluation ends with practical recommendations and an improvement plan, not just a report.
