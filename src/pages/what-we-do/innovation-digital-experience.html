Innovation & Digital Experience

Residents expect government services to work as well as the best apps on their phones. Too often they meet paper forms, dated portals, and processes that live in spreadsheets and inboxes, while staff work around tools that were never designed for them. The opportunity is real: a well-designed digital service saves time on both sides of the counter and builds trust every time it works.

What We Offer

Services

We bring end-to-end product design and engineering to the public sector: research with the people who use a service, product strategy, interface and design system work, and the front-end development to ship it. Our team has delivered digital products used by tens of millions of people across healthcare, logistics, media, and federal programs, and we design for accessibility, maintainability, and measurable adoption from day one.*

Product Strategy & Service Design

The challenge

New digital services often launch around how an agency is organized rather than how residents experience it. Without a clear picture of the end-to-end journey, teams build features that miss the real problem, creating the need for costly rework.

How we help

We map the full service journey with personas, journey maps, and service blueprints, then define the product strategy, priorities, and technical requirements that guide the build. You get a clear product vision and roadmap that connects what residents need with what the organization can deliver.*

User Research & Usability Testing

The challenge

Decisions made on assumptions are expensive to undo. Agencies rarely have the time or in-house capacity to talk with residents and frontline staff before committing to a design.

How we help

We plan and run interviews, field observation, concept tests, and usability studies with the people who will actually use the service, including the staff behind the counter. Findings come back as clear, prioritized recommendations, and we test again before launch so problems surface while they're still cheap to fix.*

Web & Mobile Product Design

The challenge

Confusing portals, forms, and apps push people back to phone lines and in-person visits, raising costs and frustration. Digital adoption stalls when the online experience is harder than the alternative.

How we help

We design end-to-end experiences across web, iOS, and Android, from information architecture and wireframes to high-fidelity prototypes and developer-ready specs. We measure success by outcomes like digital adoption and task completion, not just how it looks. Our track record of technological implementations has lifted digital adoption by a third on average.*

Design Systems & Accessibility

The challenge

When every team builds its own buttons, forms, and pages, digital services look and behave inconsistently, cost more to maintain, and often fall short of Section 508 and WCAG accessibility requirements.

How we help

We build design systems that pair a shared component library — in design tools and in code — with clear UX standards and documentation that product teams actually adopt. Accessibility is built into every component, from color contrast to keyboard and screen-reader support, so every new page inherits it.*

Web Application Development

The challenge

Even the best design falls short if it can't be built well. Many agencies depend on vendors for every change, and slow, fragile front ends frustrate residents and staff alike.

How we help

We build fast, modern, maintainable web applications with React, TypeScript, Node.js, GraphQL, and cloud services, backed by automated testing and continuous delivery. We work alongside your IT team or vendors, document what we build, and design for performance and long-term ownership.*

Workflow Digitization & AI Automation

The challenge

Permits, applications, audits, and intake still run on paper, spreadsheets, and email in many agencies. Manual data entry slows services down, invites errors, and keeps skilled staff from higher-value work.

How we help

We replace manual processes with guided digital workflows, including step-by-step forms, status tracking, and automated notifications, and apply AI and machine learning where they genuinely remove busywork, with people confirming the results. We've reduced months-long manual setups to mere hours, and we design every automation to be transparent and easy to trust.*
