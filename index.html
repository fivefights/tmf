Public Service Transformation Partners

We're an end-to-end management consultancy that helps public sector organizations imagine more and deliver better. We move bold ideas from paper to practice; guiding our clients from complexity to clarity, and from good intentions to measurable impact.

Our capabilities → Start a conversation
Our Mission

Ensuring public sector and social impact leaders have the cutting-edge strategies, tools, and support to perform at their best, because when they perform, communities prosper.

We exist to close the gap between intention and impact. That means rejecting templated approaches in favor of strategies that are tailored, human-centered, agile, and innovation-led.

With proven, multidisciplinary experience across public sector industries, and an insider's grasp of the nuances leaders face (the politics, the pressures, the trade-offs), we design solutions that go beyond solving problems. They build resilience, open new possibilities, and leave organizations and communities stronger than before.

Our Capabilities

Six connected practices. Most engagements draw on more than one, because strategy, structure, data, and the people doing the work rarely move independently.

01

Strategy & Transformation Consulting

We help public sector leaders translate ambitious vision and operational efficiency goals into actionable roadmaps, then stay engaged through execution to ensure strategies turn into real, measurable results.

Explore → 02

Organizational Design & Development

We align structure, roles, and decision rights to new and evolving organizational strategies, then we build the capabilities and leadership depth that makes the new operating model hold over time.

Explore → 03

Organizational Change Management

Transformation succeeds or fails due to people. We design and execute organizational and technology change plans that build buy-in, strengthen leadership alignment, and equip teams to adopt new ways of working with confidence.

Explore → 04

Data Analytics & Business Intelligence

We turn siloed data into clear, governed, decision-ready insights, build the technical infrastructure for AI integration, and develop dashboards and analytics capabilities that help leaders track performance, target resources, and demonstrate impact.

Explore → 05

Innovation & Customer Experience

We reimagine how public services and programs are designed and delivered, applying human-centered design to simplify processes, pilot new business and operating models, and improve the experiences of the people our clients serve.

Social Impact Consulting

We help mission-driven organizations create, implement, and measure programs that enable lasting community impact, connecting strategy to outcomes people can see and feel.

About Us

Lakeisha Sesay, MPA

Founder & Managing Director

The Momentum Firm was founded by Lakeisha Sesay, MPA, award-winning government and public sector strategist, public policy advisor, university instructor and management consulting expert.

Prior to founding The Momentum Firm, Lakeisha served as a North America Strategy Executive for the Public Service Industry at Accenture, where she gained a highly reputable track record of successfully leading complex and high-stakes transformation programs on behalf of c-suite public sector leaders and elected officials. Her work has consistently delivered results that matter for both organizations and communities — successfully aligning strategy, technology, innovation, and human-centered design to deliver lasting value.
