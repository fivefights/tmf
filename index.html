The Momentum Firm is an end-to-end management consultancy specializing in strategic planning, performance management, operational transformation and change management for public service organizations.

About the firm
Why The Momentum Firm

We know the system.
We also know how it can work better.

Public service organizations come with realities that seldom fit neatly into a consulting framework. Politics matter. Resources have limits. Decisions require a lengthy chain of command. And even the smartest idea has to work within the organization responsible for carrying it. We bring an insider's understanding of that environment with fresh thinking to challenge what no longer serves it.

Trust and relationship based

We lead with relationships. Leaders trust us with high-stakes work because we listen first, tell the truth, and stay accountable from start to finish.

Beloved by clients

Clients come back and recommend us to their peers, because we treat their mission as our own and make hard work feel possible.

Actual impacts and results

We set success measures unique to each partner and track what actually changes for the organization and the people relying on it.

Requested for expansion

Once partners see what the work delivers, they ask us to extend it into new departments, programs, and phases.

Internationally recognized results

Our work spans more than six countries and institutions such as the United Nations and the European Union, with results recognized well beyond any one city or state.
