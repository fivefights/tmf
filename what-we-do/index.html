What We Do

The proactive work behind high-performing public service.

The Momentum Firm helps public-sector organizations tackle the work that defines how well they operate today — and how ready they are for what comes next. From enterprise transformation and major program delivery to organizational change, data, and digital modernization, we bring practical thinking and hands-on support to the priorities that can't afford to lose momentum.

Capability 01

Enterprise Strategy & Transformation

Public-sector leaders are asked to deliver ambitious agendas with finite budgets, aging operating models, and priorities that shift with every budget cycle and election. The vision is rarely the problem. Turning it into a plan the whole organization can execute — and staying with it — is.

We help leaders translate vision and efficiency goals into actionable roadmaps, then stay engaged through execution so strategy turns into measurable results. From strategic plans and organizational assessments to structure, budgeting, and governance, we connect what the organization intends to achieve with how it is organized, funded, and run.*

Strategic Planning

The challenge

Many state and local governments have strategic plans that end up on a shelf: too broad to guide daily decisions, or disconnected from budgets and performance measures. Leaders need a plan that sets clear priorities and survives leadership changes and budget cycles.

How we help

We gather leadership and stakeholder input, translate it into a focused set of goals, strategies, and measurable outcomes, and build the implementation roadmap, ownership, and metrics behind each one. The result is a public-sector strategic plan your teams can actually execute, with a cadence for tracking progress against it.*

Organizational Assessments

The challenge

Before leaders can fix what's slowing an agency down, they need an honest picture of how it really operates: where processes break down, where capacity is stretched, and where resources don't match priorities.

How we help

Our organizational assessments combine data analysis, process review, staff interviews, and peer benchmarking to diagnose root causes, not symptoms. You get a clear findings report, prioritized recommendations, and the quick wins and longer-term moves to act on first.*

Organizational Design

The challenge

When roles overlap, reporting lines are unclear, or the structure no longer fits the mission, the organization feels it: slow decisions, duplicated effort, and talented people working around the system.

How we help

We design operating models and organizational structures around the services you need to deliver now and the capabilities you'll need next. Work products include current- and future-state org charts, role definitions, span-of-control analysis, and a phased transition plan that keeps services running while the change takes hold.*

Outcome-Based Budgeting

The challenge

Incremental budgets fund what was funded last year. For cities, counties, and states facing tight revenue and rising demand, that makes it hard to show residents what their dollars achieve — or to shift resources toward what works.

How we help

We help finance and executive teams link budget decisions to measurable results: defining priority outcomes, building program inventories and performance measures, and designing the process and tools to weigh funding requests against them. The result is a budget that tells a clear story about value, and a framework you can repeat every cycle.*

Enterprise Governance

The challenge

Large public organizations often struggle to make cross-department decisions quickly. Without clear decision rights and oversight, priorities compete, investments overlap, and accountability blurs.

How we help

We design enterprise governance frameworks that clarify who decides what, how priorities are set, and how progress is monitored: governance charters, decision-rights matrices, portfolio prioritization criteria, and executive steering structures. They're built to speed decisions up, not slow them down.*

Capability 02

Analytics & Operational Intelligence

Public organizations collect enormous amounts of data, but it often lives in disconnected systems, arrives too late, or can't be trusted enough to act on. Meanwhile residents, councils, and oversight bodies expect leaders to show results — and AI raises the bar for what good data foundations look like.

We turn siloed data into clear, governed, decision-ready insight. We build the data infrastructure for AI integration and develop dashboards and analytics capabilities that help leaders track operational performance, target resources, and demonstrate impact.*

Performance Dashboards & Monitoring

The challenge

Leaders shouldn't have to dig through pages of reports to know whether services are on track. Without timely, visual performance data, problems surface late and progress is hard to communicate to residents and elected officials.

How we help

We define the right performance measures, connect the underlying data, and design executive and operational dashboards that make trends, risks, and results easy to see. Then we set up the routines — targets, review meetings, and escalation paths — that turn a government performance dashboard into a management tool.*

Enterprise Data Strategy & Readiness

The challenge

Data people can't find, trust, or share has little value. Many agencies are under pressure to adopt AI and analytics before their data governance, quality, and infrastructure are ready to support it.

How we help

We assess your data maturity, then build a practical public-sector data strategy: governance roles and policies, data quality standards, an architecture and integration roadmap, and the readiness steps AI requires. The focus is sequencing — what to fix first so every investment pays off.*

AI & Self-Service Analytics

The challenge

Teams need answers faster than traditional reporting cycles allow, and leaders want to use AI responsibly to work smarter. Without the right tools, guardrails, and skills, both efforts stall.

How we help

We identify high-value AI and analytics use cases, help select and configure self-service tools, and put responsible-AI guardrails in place. Then we train staff to use them, so more people get trustworthy answers on their own instead of waiting for the next report.*

Capability 03

Program & Implementation Management

Large-scale public initiatives bring many moving parts: multiple vendors, competing interests, public scrutiny, and significant budget risk. Programs rarely fail for lack of a plan. They fail in the coordination, when issues surface too late to fix cheaply.

We oversee large organizational and technology initiatives from launch to close, working on the client's side of the table. We establish the governance, integrated planning, risk management, and stakeholder reporting that keep complex, multi-workstream programs on time, on budget, and aligned to mission outcomes.*

Major Initiative Management

The challenge

Signature initiatives — a mayoral priority, a new statewide program, a large reorganization — carry high visibility and little room for delay. They cut across departments that don't usually work together.

How we help

We provide hands-on program leadership: integrated work plans, dependency and risk management, issue resolution, and executive-ready status reporting. Every workstream stays connected to the outcome the initiative was launched to deliver, and leaders get an early view of anything that threatens it.*

Technology Implementation Oversight

The challenge

Major technology implementations — ERP, permitting, case management, CRM — can look on track while risks build behind the scenes. Agencies often lack the independent, technical view needed to hold vendors accountable.

How we help

We act as your independent advocate through implementation: reviewing vendor plans, deliverables, and testing; tracking scope, schedule, and budget; and surfacing risks early with clear recommendations. Our IV&V-style oversight protects the investment and makes sure the system that goes live works for staff and residents.*

PMO Setup & Support

The challenge

As portfolios grow, organizations need a consistent way to prioritize, plan, and report on projects. Without one, every project runs differently and leaders lack a reliable view of the whole.

How we help

We design and stand up program and project management offices sized to your organization: intake and governance processes, standard templates and tools, portfolio reporting, and role definitions. We can run the PMO alongside your team while building the internal capability to sustain it.*

Capability 04

Organizational Change Management

Transformation succeeds or fails because of people. New systems, structures, and leadership priorities only deliver value when the employees expected to carry them are ready, aligned, and supported — and when the communities affected understand what's changing and why.

We design and execute organizational and technology change plans that build buy-in, strengthen leadership alignment, and equip teams to adopt new ways of working with confidence. Our approach is grounded in public-sector realities: civil service structures, labor relations, public accountability, and leadership turnover.*

Leadership & Administration Transitions

The challenge

A new mayor, governor, superintendent, or agency head brings new priorities on a tight clock. Transitions put continuity at risk just as expectations are highest.

How we help

We support transitions from both sides: briefing books, priority-setting, and first-100-days plans for incoming leaders, and continuity planning for the teams who keep services running. The goal is a fast, steady start that turns new priorities into an actionable agenda.*

Change Planning & Execution

The challenge

Significant change touches roles, workflows, and day-to-day operations all at once. Without a deliberate plan, resistance builds, productivity dips, and the benefits of the investment never fully arrive.

How we help

We assess change impacts and readiness, then build and execute the change management plan: sponsor roadmaps, change-agent networks, communications, and resistance management. We measure adoption along the way, so leaders know where to focus before issues become setbacks.*

Training & Adoption Support

The challenge

A new system or process is only as good as the people using it. Generic training rarely fits the realities of frontline public-sector work, and adoption fades once go-live support ends.

How we help

We design role-based training, job aids, and learning materials around how your staff actually work, delivered in person, virtually, or self-paced. Post-launch adoption support and usage tracking help new ways of working stick.*

Stakeholder & Community Engagement

The challenge

Public-sector change affects residents, employees, unions, elected officials, and community partners, each with different concerns. Decisions made without meaningful engagement lose trust and momentum.

How we help

We map stakeholders and design engagement that opens real two-way communication: community meetings, listening sessions, surveys, advisory groups, and clear public messaging. We turn what we hear into insight leaders can use, and close the loop so people see how their input shaped the outcome.*

Capability 05

Innovation & Digital Experience

Residents expect government services to be as easy to use as the best digital experiences in their lives. Many public organizations are still working with legacy systems, manual processes, and programs designed around the institution rather than the people they serve.

We redesign systems, services, and experiences to be more effective for the people who use them. Using human-centered design, rapid prototyping, and evaluation, we help clients test new ideas before scaling them, modernize legacy workflows and constituent services, and make confident technology decisions — so they deliver faster, more seamless public services.*

Innovation Sprints & Design Workshops

The challenge

Agencies face problems existing approaches haven't solved, but rarely have the time or structure to explore new ones. Ideas stall in meetings without a way to test them quickly.

How we help

We run design thinking workshops and time-boxed innovation sprints that take teams from problem framing through research, ideation, and rapid prototyping in days or weeks, not months. Teams leave with tested concepts, clear next steps, and the skills to repeat the process themselves.*

New Program Design & Pilots

The challenge

New funding, mandates, or community needs often require launching programs quickly. Programs designed without the people they serve, or launched at full scale untested, risk low uptake and costly fixes.

How we help

We co-design programs with residents and frontline staff, define the logic model and success measures, and launch through structured pilots that test assumptions before full rollout. You get a program blueprint, operating procedures, and pilot results that make the case for scaling.*

Program Evaluation & Improvement

The challenge

Funders, councils, and residents want evidence that programs work. Many agencies lack the capacity to evaluate rigorously, so it's hard to know what to expand, fix, or retire.

How we help

We design and conduct program evaluations, from logic models and evaluation plans to data collection, analysis, and findings, using methods suited to the program and its budget. Every evaluation ends with practical recommendations and an improvement plan, not just a report.*

311 & Constituent Services Modernization

The challenge

For many residents, 311 is where government becomes personal: the place to get an answer, request a service, or report a problem. Fragmented channels, long waits, and requests that disappear into a backlog erode trust quickly.

How we help

We modernize 311 and constituent services end to end: omnichannel strategy, service request workflows, knowledge bases, CRM requirements, and service standards. We also use 311 data to spot trends and predict demand, so the first point of contact becomes a source of insight for the whole government.*

Technology Requirements & Solution Selection

The challenge

Choosing technology is only part of the decision. Agencies that go to market without clear business requirements risk buying systems that don't fit how they work, followed by costly change orders.

How we help

We document business processes and requirements with the people who will use the system, scan the vendor market, and support procurement from RFP development through demos, scoring, and negotiation. The result is a defensible, well-documented selection that fits your needs and budget.*

Digital Product & Usability Design

The challenge

Online forms, portals, and apps that confuse people push residents back to phone lines and counters, raising costs and frustration. Accessibility and plain language are no longer optional.

How we help

We design digital services with real users: research, journey mapping, wireframes, prototypes, and usability testing, with accessibility (WCAG) and plain language built in from the start. We deliver design specifications your developers or vendors can build from, and iterate based on how people actually use the product.*
